"Explain Artificial Intelligence and rational decision systems. How does a rational agent differ from human-like cognitive heuristic models under uncertain dynamic environments?"

Student Typed Response Words: 184 • Characters: 1,180

Artificial Intelligence can be formalized as the study and design of intelligent agents capable of perceiving their operational environment through sensors and acting upon it using actuators.

A rational decision system strictly selects actions that maximize expected performance outcomes given its perceptual history and built-in knowledge base, adhering to mathematical utility theory.

In contrast, human cognitive heuristic models often rely on satisficing, cognitive biases, and emotional shortcuts. Under uncertain dynamic environments, rational agents evaluate probabilistic state transitions (e.g., Markov Decision Processes), whereas human heuristics prioritize computational economy over theoretical optimality.
